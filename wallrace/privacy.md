# Privacy Policy

Wallrace Legal  
Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what Wallrace does with information. In short: Wallrace does not collect, store or share any personal information.

## 1. Who We Are

Wallrace is operated by Vexellab Solutions LLP. Wallrace is a turn-based board game for one to four players on a single device, played against other people passing the device or against computer opponents.

## 2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

- There are no accounts, sign-in or profiles.
- There is no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.
- Matches are played entirely on your device. Wallrace does not send gameplay or device information to us or to anyone else.
- Player names in a match are generated by the app (such as "Blue" or "Gold bot") and never leave the device.

## 3. Information Stored On Your Device

Wallrace stores one preference on your device: whether sound effects are on or off. It is kept in your device's local app storage, is never transmitted, and is removed when you uninstall the app.

## 4. Network Access

Wallrace does not need an internet connection to play. The only time it uses the network is when you choose to open this Privacy Policy or the Terms of Use, which open in your device's web browser. Those pages are served by our website host, which may process standard web request information (such as IP address) under its own policies.

## 5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not collect any.

## 6. Children

Wallrace is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

## 7. Your Choices

- You can turn sound effects on or off in Options.
- You can remove all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling Wallrace.

## 8. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Wallrace changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If Wallrace ever starts collecting information, we will update this policy before that change ships.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Vexellab through the [main website contact page](https://www.vexellab.com/#contact).
