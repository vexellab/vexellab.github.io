# Delete Your Wallrace Account

Wallrace Legal  
Last updated: October 3, 2026

A Wallrace account is only used for online play. Deleting it permanently removes your account, your email address, your display name, the rooms you created and your seats in other players' rooms. Play on your own device is not affected.

## Delete In The App

1. Open Wallrace and tap the Options button on the home screen.
2. Tap your account, then **Delete account**.
3. Enter your password and tap **Delete forever**.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

## If You Can't Open The App

Contact Vexellab through the [main website contact page](https://www.vexellab.com/#contact) from the email address on the account and ask us to delete your Wallrace account. We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.
