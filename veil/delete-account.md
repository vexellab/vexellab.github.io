# Account Deletion

VEIL Legal  
Last updated: June 13, 2026

Veil lets members request account deletion directly inside the app. This page explains how to do that and what happens next.

## Delete Your Account In The App

1. Open Veil and sign in to the account you want to delete.
2. Go to **Profile**.
3. Tap **Delete Account**.
4. Enter a reason for leaving.
5. Confirm the deletion request.

## What Is Deleted

- Your Veil profile is marked deleted and removed from active member discovery.
- Your followers, following relationships, follow requests, and private suggestions are deleted.
- Your private library-only activity, including items without a public review, is deleted.

## What May Remain

- Public reviews, comments, and reactions may remain visible inside Veil.
- Any retained public contribution is anonymized and shown as **Deleted user**.
- Limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or backup recovery.

## Timing

Deletion is initiated immediately after confirmation in the app. Residual backup or infrastructure records may take additional time to expire in the ordinary course of system maintenance.

## Need Help?

If you cannot access the app or need help with deletion, contact Vexellab through the [main website contact page](https://www.vexellab.com/#contact) and include the email address associated with your Veil account.
