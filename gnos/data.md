# Your Data & Deletion

Gnos Legal

Last updated: November 7, 2026

The public Gnos song-review experience uses an account. Reviews, ratings and
favorites are stored on our server, not just on your device. Signing out,
clearing device storage or uninstalling Gnos does **not** delete your account
or its server-held reviews and favorites.

## Delete your account in the app

1. Sign in to the Gnos account you want to delete.
2. Open the **Profile** tab.
3. Select **Delete account**.
4. Read the confirmation, enter your **Current password**, then select
   **Delete account** in the dialog.
5. If the request fails, check the displayed error and retry when the service is
   available. Do not assume an unsuccessful request completed deletion.

This action cannot be undone. A successful request deletes the account and its
associated reviews, ratings, favorites, account sessions and email-action tokens
from the active database. Removing reviews also changes the related aggregate
ratings and counts. It does not delete songs or artwork at the catalog source,
a Google/YouTube account, or copies other users have made of public reviews.

If you have forgotten your password, use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in
page to request a password-reset email before trying again. Do not send your
password or reset/verification links to support.

## Manage individual information

| Information | Current control |
| --- | --- |
| Display name | **Profile → Your name**, edit the name and select **Save**. This is the name shown with community reviews, not a separate unique username. |
| Rating or review text | Open the song from **My Reviews** or **Discover**, edit your rating/text and select **Update review**. |
| Entire rating/review | Open the song, select **Delete** for your review and confirm **Delete**. This removes both the rating and optional text. |
| Favorite | Open the song from **Favorites** or **Discover** and tap its selected heart to remove it. |
| Current sign-in session | **Profile → Sign out**, then confirm **Sign out**. Your reviews and favorites stay with your account. |

Your reviews and display name are community-visible to other eligible signed-in
users. Favorites are returned for your own account and are not published as a
community favorites list. Do not put sensitive personal information into a
public review or display name.

## If you cannot use the app

Use [Contact Vexellab](https://www.vexellab.com/#contact), also available through
**Profile → Contact**, to request account/data deletion or help with access.
Identify **Gnos**, the email address associated with the account and what you
want deleted. We may need to verify account ownership before acting. For
support messages or other information you previously sent us, describe the
relevant correspondence. This is a support request route, not an automatic
web-based account-deletion form.

The app does not currently provide a dedicated data-export button. Use the same
contact route for access, correction or other privacy requests.

## Local data and retained copies

Gnos stores account-session information and a generated device identity in
platform secure storage, and may retain preferences, artwork caches and catalog
session data locally. Sign-out removes the stored account session, not every
local item. Use available operating-system app-data controls for remaining
local data. On iOS, offloading can retain documents and data, and secure-storage
items can persist separately. Device backups or restoration can also retain or
restore information. Do not rely on uninstalling alone to erase every copy.

Account deletion removes the active account records described above, but is not
a promise of immediate erasure from every system. Administrative audit records
can remain with their account actor link removed. Backend cleanup routines have
separate eligibility periods for tokens, sessions and audit/operational records.
Server logs, backups, support correspondence and email-provider records may
remain separately. See the [Privacy Policy](/gnos/privacy/) for those retention
limits. We do not promise a fixed backup or log deletion deadline.

## Third-party information and reporting

Catalog/search and artwork providers receive network requests even if no review
or favorite is saved. Deleting Gnos account data does not erase requests held by
those providers. Use their own privacy/account controls, including the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for Google services.

To report an objectionable review or privacy/rights concern, use **Profile →
Contact** or the [Vexellab contact page](https://www.vexellab.com/#contact).
Include the song, reviewer's displayed name, the review text or screenshot and
the reason for your concern. The current experience does not have a dedicated
in-app report or user-blocking button.
