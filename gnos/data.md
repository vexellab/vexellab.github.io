# Your Data & Deletion

Gnos Legal

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Gnos currently has no Vexellab account to delete. Your saved library and listening
preferences are stored on your device. You can manage them from **Gnos → Settings
→ Storage & privacy**.

## Choose what to clear

| Control | What it removes |
| --- | --- |
| Clear search history | The current recent-played search keywords and legacy recent-query records on this device. |
| Clear listening history | Recent plays and listening-based recommendation statistics on this device. New listening can create new history. |
| Clear artwork cache | Cached cover images. Images may download again when needed. |
| Reset library | Explicit favorites, saved albums/artists/playlists, local playlists, and stored collection-track metadata. |

Reset library is destructive and requires confirmation. It does not remove songs
from YouTube or delete a provider account. Each control covers its named data
category; clearing history does not reset your saved library. Currently playing
queue entries may remain in memory until playback/app use ends.

## Location and browser data

To stop new location access, change Gnos’s permission in your device’s privacy
or app settings. A previously saved broad discovery area may remain locally.

To remove all remaining app preferences, saved area information, and embedded
browser data, use your device’s app-data removal controls or delete the app.
On iOS, **Delete App** differs from **Offload App**, which can retain documents
and data. On Android, storage/app-data controls vary by device manufacturer.
Backups or later device restoration can restore previously backed-up data.

## Third-party accounts and information

Deleting Gnos or clearing its local data does not delete your Google/YouTube
account or information held by another provider. If you used provider sign-in
through the source browser, use the provider’s own account and session controls.
For Google-held information, see [Google Account](https://myaccount.google.com/)
and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Search and playback requests are transmitted to source services even when they
are not saved in Gnos’s local history. Providers may retain those requests under
their own policies.

## Support and privacy requests

For information you sent to Vexellab support, or questions about these controls,
contact **Vexellab Solutions LLP** through the
[Vexellab contact page](https://www.vexellab.com/#contact). Identify Gnos and the
request you want help with. Do not send account passwords or signed media URLs.

Vexellab cannot remotely retrieve or erase library information that exists only
on your device. See the [Privacy Policy](/gnos/privacy/) for retention and rights
information and the [Terms & Conditions](/gnos/terms/) for service terms.
