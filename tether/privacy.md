# Privacy Policy

Tether Legal  
Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Tether handles. In short: you can play without an account. Optional analytics stay off unless you turn them on. If you sign in, we keep the minimum needed to sync your progress, run daily leaderboards and honour your purchases. Tether shows ads from Google AdMob unless you buy Remove Ads.

## 1. Who We Are

Tether is a puzzle game operated by Vexellab Solutions LLP ("Vexellab", "we", "us"). You fill every light cell of a board with rune-anchored pieces across hand-made worlds, daily challenges and events.

## 2. Playing Without An Account

Campaign levels, daily challenges, events and settings work without an account. Your progress, stars, streaks, hint balance and settings are stored on your device.

## 3. Information We Collect If You Sign In

Signing in with Apple or Google is optional. It lets you sync progress across devices, appear on daily leaderboards and buy items. If you sign in, we handle:

- **Account identifiers** — a Firebase user ID, your email address and, if your provider shares it, your name. Apple users may choose to hide their email with Apple's private relay address.
- **Profile** — the display name and profile mark you choose in Tether.
- **Game progress** — stars, solved levels, streaks, daily history, hint token balance and owned themes, so we can sync them between devices.
- **Daily challenge results** — your move count and stars for each daily puzzle, used to rank the daily leaderboard.
- **Purchase records** — product IDs and transaction events for items you buy, so we can deliver hint tokens and restore purchases.
- **Technical information** — standard server logs such as IP address and request times, kept briefly for security and to keep the service running.

## 4. Analytics (Off Unless You Turn It On)

Tether can send pseudonymous gameplay events (for example, level started or finished, moves used, hints used and ad outcomes) to Google Firebase Analytics to help us balance puzzles. This is **off by default** and only runs if you enable "Share pseudonymous usage analytics" in Settings. It never includes your name, email or board layouts, and ad personalisation signals are disabled. You can turn it off at any time.

## 5. Advertising

Tether shows rewarded ads (only when you choose to watch one for a deeper hint) and occasional ads between levels, served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device information such as advertising identifiers, IP address and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), Tether asks for your consent through Google's consent form before requesting ads, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Buying **Remove Ads** turns off ads between levels. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses information from apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 6. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and keep track of what you own; RevenueCat receives your Firebase user ID, product IDs and transaction details. See [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## 7. How We Use Information

- To sign you in and sync your progress between devices.
- To run daily challenges and leaderboards.
- To deliver and restore purchases and hint tokens.
- To show ads and, with your consent, measure how the game is played.
- To prevent abuse, fix problems and keep the service reliable.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers listed in this policy (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, RevenueCat and our hosting provider) acting for us, or where the law requires it.

## 8. What Other Players See

Daily leaderboards show your display name, profile mark, moves and stars. Other players never see your email address.

## 9. Information Stored On Your Device

Tether stores your progress, settings, analytics choice and content downloads on your device. If you sign in, Firebase keeps a sign-in token on your device. Uninstalling Tether removes this data.

## 10. Data Retention And Deletion

- Device-only data stays on your device until you reset progress in Settings or uninstall Tether.
- Account data is kept until you delete your account.
- You can delete your account at any time in Tether: Settings → your profile → **Delete account**. This permanently deletes your sign-in account, profile, synced progress, daily results, leaderboard entries, hint token balance and purchase records held by us, revokes Sign in with Apple access where used, and clears Tether data on that device.
- Purchases made through Apple or Google remain in your store account and can be restored after signing in again.
- If you can no longer open the app, see [Delete Account](/tether/delete-account/).

## 11. Security

Network traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never receive your Apple or Google password. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

## 12. Children

Tether is suitable for a general audience but is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## 13. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA or India's DPDP Act), you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict or delete your information and to withdraw consent. You can change analytics and ad choices and delete your account in the app; for anything else, contact us.

## 14. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Tether changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.

## 15. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Vexellab through the [main website contact page](https://www.vexellab.com/#contact).
