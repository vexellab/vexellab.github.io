# Privacy Policy

Deload Legal  
Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Deload handles. In short: you need an account to plan and log training. We keep your training and the profile details you give us so the app can build your plan, log your workouts and show your progress. Deload has no ads and no analytics or tracking, and we never sell your information.

## 1. Who We Are

Deload is a strength-training app operated by Vexellab Solutions LLP ("Vexellab", "we", "us"). It helps you follow a training program, log your sets and see your progress.

## 2. Information We Collect

**Account**

- Your email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash and we never see them in plain text.
- The display name you choose.

**Fitness profile**, from onboarding and Settings. Fields marked optional can be skipped.

- Training goal, interests, experience level, available equipment, training days, session length, units and week start.
- Year of birth, height and body weight.
- Sex and target weight (both optional). Sex is used only to estimate calories and to choose the body figure shown on muscle maps.

**Training data**

- The programs you follow, save or build, and your workout schedule.
- Your workouts: exercises, sets, weight, reps, durations, rest times and how hard a session felt.
- Records, achievements, streaks and favourite exercises worked out from your workouts.
- Body-weight entries you log.

**Technical information**

- Standard server logs such as IP address and request times, kept briefly for security and to keep the service running.
- Crash and error reports, described in section 5.

Your fitness profile and training data are health and fitness information. We use them only to provide Deload's features to you.

## 3. Before You Sign Up

You can answer the onboarding questions before creating an account. Those answers stay on your device until you sign up, then they become your fitness profile.

## 4. How We Use Information

- To create and secure your account, sign you in and send account emails such as password resets.
- To recommend programs that suit your goal, experience, equipment and schedule.
- To schedule your workouts, log your training and calculate your progress, records and achievements.
- To sync your data between your devices.
- To fix problems, prevent abuse and keep the service reliable.

We do not use your information for advertising. We do not build marketing profiles, and we do not sell or rent your personal information.

## 5. Crash Reports

When Deload crashes or hits an error, it sends a technical report to Sentry, our error-monitoring provider. Reports include things like the app version, device model, operating system and the technical details of the error. Deload is set up so that Sentry does not receive personal information such as your name or email address. See [Sentry's privacy policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/).

## 6. Who We Share Information With

We share information only with the service providers that run Deload for us, under contracts that limit how they may use it:

- **Our hosting provider**, for the servers that store your account and training data.
- **An email delivery provider**, to send account emails.
- **Sentry**, for crash and error reports.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

## 7. Information Stored On Your Device

- Deload saves your workouts on your device first, so logging works offline, and syncs them when you are back online.
- It keeps a copy of the exercise library on your device.
- Your sign-in tokens are kept in the device's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android).
- Rest-timer and workout reminder notifications are scheduled on your device.

Uninstalling Deload removes this data from the device. Your account data stays on our servers until you delete your account.

## 8. Data Retention And Deletion

- We keep your account and training data for as long as your account exists.
- When you delete your account, it is deactivated immediately: you are signed out on every device and can no longer sign in.
- Your account and all related data are then permanently erased from our systems within 30 days.
- To delete your account, see [Delete Account](/deload/delete-account/).

## 9. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS), and passwords are stored only as secure hashes. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

## 10. Children

Deload is not directed to children under 13, and you must be at least 13 years old to use it. Users aged 13 to 15 should train under adult supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## 11. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA or India's DPDP Act), you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict or delete your information and to withdraw consent. You can edit your profile and preferences in the app. For a copy of your data or anything else, contact us.

## 12. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Deload changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.

## 13. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Vexellab through the [main website contact page](https://www.vexellab.com/#contact).
