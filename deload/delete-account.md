# Delete Your Deload Account

Deload Legal  
Last updated: October 20, 2026

Deleting your Deload account permanently removes your account and everything linked to it, including:

- your email address and display name
- your fitness profile
- programs you follow, saved or built
- your workout schedule, logged workouts and sets
- records, achievements and body-weight entries

Your account is deactivated as soon as we process the request: you are signed out on every device and can no longer sign in. Your data is then permanently erased from our systems within 30 days. This cannot be undone.

## Delete In The App

1. Open Deload, sign in and go to the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap **Settings** (the gear at the top right).
3. Under **Account**, tap **Delete account**.
4. Read the message, then tap **Continue**.
5. Enter your password and tap **Delete account**.

You are signed out right away, and the account's data on that device is removed.

## Request Deletion

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in, contact Vexellab through the [main website contact page](https://www.vexellab.com/#contact) from the email address you use for Deload, and ask us to delete your Deload account. We will confirm, then delete the account and its data within 30 days.

To keep a copy of your data first, say so in your message and we will send it to you before deleting.
